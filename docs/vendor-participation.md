# Voluntary vendor participation

ReceptionBench welcomes preparation by teams building receptionist and voice-agent systems. Participation is currently local preparation: the offline core uses a fake adapter, M2 integration is underway and no verified leaderboard, live submission endpoint or certification is available. Use [the project issues](https://github.com/pmannle/receptionbench/issues/new/choose) or [phil.mannle@gmail.com](mailto:phil.mannle@gmail.com) to propose participation. Downloading a brief from the local website does not enroll a team or send information.

## Prepare a participation brief

Record your team/system, desired capability profile, supported and unsupported tasks, version/configuration identifiers, adapter approach and the evidence you can make available. Begin with synthetic English-language reception and salon business facts. Broader domains require their own reviewed packs; current coverage does not establish cross-industry performance.

Explain how recipient outcomes will be observed independently of vendor telemetry. Booking-link handoff, actual booking mutation, callback request/execution and transfer/media/return behavior are distinct capabilities. Include local reproduction and ownership-checked cleanup instructions. Share only synthetic, redistributable examples and appropriately redacted diagnostics through the project issues or participation email.

## Common evaluation rules

All systems must receive the same published policies, preparation time, suite definitions, allowed outcomes, repetitions and failure treatment for a comparable cohort. Do not prescribe a vendor's internal tool sequence. Freeze and digest target configuration before the evaluation window. Report opaque model/vendor versions as opaque; disclose capability exclusions and infrastructure replacements.

Evidence determines the outcome. A vendor's own success response is useful diagnostic data, but cannot independently establish a recipient receipt, completed appointment or working audio bridge. Missing required evidence cannot pass. Unsupported required capabilities, vendor operational failures and benchmark infrastructure failures must remain visible under the published treatment; preserve original attempts.

Future reported results must identify suite/evaluator/adapter/profile/transport versions, dates, source/configuration digests, sample counts, scope, evidence coverage, critical violations and uncertainty. Simulation, self-reported, benchmark-operated and independently audited evidence must be distinguished. No single aggregate score should conceal these fields.

## Conflicts and independent operation

Receptionaly is the initial reference system. Its developers must not administer the active hidden case bank. A second non-Receptionaly vendor adapter, a second domain and independent hidden-test custody are pending. Until independent operation is demonstrated, results must be labelled internal research rather than independent certification.

The planned governance includes independent scenario approval, conflicts disclosures, access logs, precommitted suites, contamination handling, appeals and versioned rescoring. Evaluator corrections must apply consistently to affected systems, with prior reports retained. Sponsorship must not change inclusion, cases or scoring. These are requirements for future independent evaluation, not claims that the governance program already exists.

## Before a controlled live run

A separate run agreement must fix controlled targets, consent/recording requirements, evidence access, retention, privacy handling, vendor configuration, capability profile, sample size, costs, allowed retries and stop/cleanup rules. No live call, external message, paid provider use or public result is authorized by preparing a brief or adapter. Default development remains local and offline.

## Before publication

The owner approved public source/website release, Apache-2.0 code/documentation and CC0-1.0 synthetic fixture/scenario data. See [provenance](provenance.md). Source preparation does not establish a completed deployment. For participant results, agree evidence access/redaction, reproducibility artifacts and publication scope separately. Participation is voluntary. No team is listed, ranked or represented as endorsing ReceptionBench merely because it contributed code or reviewed a case.
