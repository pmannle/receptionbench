02 / WHAT MAKES A COMPARISON

Same task.
Same conditions.

Matched evaluation cohorts

Compare the same suite, evaluator, capability profile, transport, language, caller policy and observation mode. An SMS receipt test and a transfer-media test belong to different cohorts.

Every attempt accounted for

Publish sample counts, failures, unsupported tasks, infrastructure errors, completion and uncertainty. A single successful call is a smoke test, not an aggregate rank.

Evidence and independence

Distinguish self-reported, benchmark-operated and independently audited evidence. Receptionaly is the initial reference system; its developer’s results are labelled internal research with the relationship disclosed.

Review before publication

Entries require publication permission, configuration and evidence digests, review provenance, scope and cost limitations. Raw phone numbers, private recordings and credentials stay private.

Numerical ranking opens after reviewed cohorts, repeated trials, completion gates and uncertainty reporting are implemented. Independent certification additionally requires independent operation and hidden-test custody.
